Paneer Fried Rice

A flavorful Indo-Chinese style fried rice with soft paneer cubes, crisp vegetables, and aromatic sauces.

Vegetarian Rice Dish Quick Meal Street Style
2

Ingredients

    Instructions

    1. Prepare rice

      Use cooked and cooled rice. Ensure grains are separate.

    2. Cook paneer

      Lightly sauté paneer cubes in a little oil until slightly golden. Set aside.

    3. Stir-fry vegetables

      Heat oil in a wok. Add garlic, then vegetables. Stir-fry on high heat.

    4. Add sauces

      Add soy sauce, chili sauce, pepper, and salt. Mix well.

    5. Combine rice

      Add rice and paneer. Toss gently without breaking rice.

    6. Finish

      Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.
